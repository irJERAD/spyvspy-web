Every roll carries ±8 points of luck. Naming the wrong room is an automatic miss, however close you were. The pistol is aimed a round in advance, so a spy who moves after you declare is a spy you cannot hit.
Blade — the sneak
Two wounds. Reaches one room. A hit puts you straight back into the shadows; a miss leaves you standing in the open for a round. The move that rewards patience.
Pistol — the loud one
Five wounds, four rooms of reach, and it fires a full round after you declare it. You carry three rounds and reload one each round you spend hidden. You are visible the whole time and cannot hide. You are betting they stay put.
Trap — the tripwire
Spend a round stringing a wire across your room. If he ends any round standing there, it springs: three wounds, and he is seen. You keep one trap at a time; setting another moves it. He cannot see yours, and you cannot see his unless you Listen close enough.
Listen — the tell
Spends your round to learn where a hidden spy is standing, within two rooms, and whether a pistol is being levelled at you. Information as a weapon.
